Question this prototype folder was answering: what is the conceptual spine of the site
pivot, such that the design is *derived* from it rather than mixed-and-matched?

Process: 2 diagnosticians (mockup audit + her-own-language audit) → 3 proposers with
different starting commitments → 3 hostile auditors (one verified against the repos) →
defense/revision round. Divya's verdict on the four visual mockups: A and D had
"something interesting happening"; B and C were mix-and-match.

## Established by diagnosis (ground truth)

- Her stable, never-cut material is a MECHANISM CHAIN, not a theme:
  make you a participant → the glass is gone → you can't judge or detach →
  you catch yourself → you look at what you'd have walked past.
- Derivation test that separates real from mix-and-match: a decision is derived iff
  the container is constructed from the item's own material ("metadata becomes material").
- A's failure: live-but-uniform (one field standing for eleven works).
  D's failure: specific-but-inert (dioramas + outbound clicks = display glass).
- Her nouns: "things" (identity), "pieces" (formal). Never "experiences"/"builds";
  "worlds" occurs once. Play-register and boundary-poetics are dead for self-description.
- (un)seen asymmetry: interactive works act on viewers; (un)seen acts on HER first.

## The converged law (all three revised spines agree — treat as settled)

1. **Seduction before catch.** Labels and allure up front (Bazaar brands before you pick);
   the visitor's chosen act is the hinge. Label-withholding/mystery-meat: killed.
2. **Container from contents.** Every work presented via its own material, on a ladder:
   the work running (itch embeds, web builds) → its rule run small → real fragments/scans →
   her first-person testimony (for un-excerptable client work). Never a descriptive card
   where a higher tier is materially available. No scenery containers (fake props, uniform
   card grids, gallery metaphors as default).
3. **Respond, never perform.** Nothing moves unprompted. No autoplay, no attract loops.
   Reduced-motion strips animation, not interaction — picks/reveals become instant state
   changes, so the proof survives.
4. **No verdicts; residue instead.** The site never reports/scores the visitor's conduct
   (the "you walked past nineteen" tally was killed: catcher/moment/affect all inverted vs
   her catch). Opened/closed states persist visibly as traces of the visitor's own acts —
   discoverable, never announced.
5. **No dead affordances.** Nothing touchable that doesn't answer; no outbound door
   without a destination; in-progress stated as data.
6. **Facts stay flat.** CV, talks, education, contact: plain dated lists, no mechanism.
   The product decade is reframed as the largest evidence body for "I make interactive
   things," not biography.
7. **Looking is a real use.** Illustrations shown whole and large, no simulated mechanics.
   (un)seen presented as "a world drawn over the real one" (the derived unit from mockup D);
   the visitor never triggers the drawing (the asymmetry holds).
8. **Chrome is inert.** Nav/footer/404 carry no mechanism. Identity sentence appears once,
   plainly, at arrival, next to demonstration.
9. **Friends page survives whole** — it is a thing she made; warmth is hers.
10. Verified facts: no script sources for the 2020 games exist (embeds ARE the works —
    tier 0, cheap); Metro's records.json (24,646) is real but needs anonymization before
    any record surfaces on-site; the corpus is majority looked-at work.

## The remaining decision (Divya's call)

**What is the site-level container?** Two survivors:

- **Works-forward flow** (Made of Itself v2): home IS the works — one flow of self-shaped,
  unequal containers, flagship first (Metro dataset piece), with about/CV as flat pages.
  Safest strong option.
- **One whole-person record** (One Record of Use v2): one flat list of everything since
  physics — works, Klydo, talks, the site itself — **ordered by last use** (the living end
  is the front door; juvenilia sinks by data, not curation). No About page; biography
  exists as entries + a printable flat export. Most distinctive; carries the whole-person
  claim structurally.

These can merge (the flow of self-shaped containers *is* the record, last-used-ordered);
the open question is whether physics/Klydo/talks live IN the flow or on a flat about page.

**Stakes posture** (second, smaller call): Made-of-itself argues a miniature pick with
scarcity + irreversibility + residue is faithful (Bazaar's own stakes are synthetic —
scarcity, not price). Working-slivers argues the index catch is real but smaller than the
works' catch, and the site shouldn't overclaim. Both agree residue is the mechanism.
